A calm, type-led theme for cleaning companies and other local service businesses.

== Description ==

Sashwell Lite is built for local service businesses: cleaners, gardeners, trades and small offices.
It puts the phone number and the areas you cover where people look for them, and lets the rest
of the page be calm type, hairlines and white space.

* Header with a top bar (phone, email, note), dropdown and big menus, search and a quote button.
* Side panel with your contact details, opening hours, WhatsApp, the areas you cover and social links.
* Call and WhatsApp bar on phones.
* Light and dark mode, chosen by the visitor with one button.
* Quiet motion that switches off with the visitor's "reduce motion" setting or the footer switch.
* Blog in a list or a grid, a featured article, reading times, topics, a contents list in long
  articles and a reading progress bar.
* Thirteen block patterns (two heroes, services as a list or with photos, about, how it works,
  prices, what is included, figures, reviews, questions, call to action and a full home page)
  and a Landing page template.
* Every option under Appearance > Customize > Theme options. No plugins needed.
* Right-to-left ready, translation ready, self-hosted fonts (no requests to other servers).

== Installation ==

1. In your admin, go to Appearance > Themes and click Add New Theme.
2. Search for "Sashwell Lite", then click Install and Activate.
3. Go to Appearance > Customize > Theme options to add your phone number, email and the rest.

= Building the home page =

1. Create a page called Home. In the page settings, choose the template "Landing page".
2. When the editor offers patterns for the new page, pick "Home page". You can also open the block
   inserter, go to Patterns > Sashwell and add the sections one by one.
3. Go to Settings > Reading, choose "A static page" and select Home as the homepage.

= Menus =

Add a menu under Appearance > Menus and assign it to "Main menu". In Screen Options, turn on
"CSS Classes" and "Description":

* `mega` on a top item turns its dropdown into a big menu: each second-level item is a column.
* `cols-2` on a top item lists its dropdown in two columns.
* `icon-<name>` on a dropdown link sets its icon (home, building, sheet, clock, chat, key, star, ...).
  Without it, an icon is picked from the link's title.
* The Description of a dropdown link is the small line under it.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does the theme need any plugins? =

No. Everything works with WordPress alone.

= Where do I change the phone number, the footer and the social links? =

Appearance > Customize > Theme options.

= How do I turn off the animations? =

Untick "Animations" under Appearance > Customize > Theme options > Footer. Visitors who set
"reduce motion" on their device never see them.
